FAQs

How do I pay?

I'll send an invoice via email with a link so you can pay securely online via credit card (via Stripe) or do a direct transfer. If doing a direct transfer make sure you use the invoice number as the reference.

What do I need for online tutoring?

A Google account with access to Google Docs and Google Meet. You'll also need a microphone and webcam.

What if I can't attend my lesson?

No problem, these things happen. Let me know which lesson you can't attend and we'll reschedule it. I don't make my students pay for lessons they don't have.

Do you tutor in person?

Not anymore, it's much for convenient for everyone to attend online.

Do I pay week by week or by school term?

It's up to you. You can prepay for 5 or 10 lessons at a time or pay as you go. I tend to send invoices monthly.

Are you a qualified teacher?

I have a Bachelor of Arts/Bachelor of Education (English Literature) from the University of New South Wales.
